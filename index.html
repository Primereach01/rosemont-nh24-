Overview

A higher way of living on NH-24

Rosemont Residency Phase 1 is a premium residential project by Aditya Group inside Aditya World City on NH-24, Ghaziabad. It offers spacious 3 BHK and 3 BHK + Servant apartments with wide balconies, contemporary architecture and large open greens.

With only four residences per floor, a 30-metre green belt and dual road frontage along NH-24, the project balances privacy with quick access to Noida, Indirapuram and East Delhi.
